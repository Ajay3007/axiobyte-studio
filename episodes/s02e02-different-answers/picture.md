# THE ONE PICTURE — s02e02

## The One Picture

One question, many futures. The next token is drawn from a spread of
probabilities — a wheel whose slices are as wide as the chances — and every
draw becomes the start of the next prediction, so one different early word
grows into a different answer. Temperature reshapes the slices before the draw.

## The counter-picture

The answer as one fixed thing the model holds and recites: ask twice, get the
same reply, and a different reply means something broke. The opening lets that
expectation fail in front of the viewer — two answers, neither a mistake.

## The payoff frame

The two answers from the opening, intact, each lit as one path through the tree
of possible answers that the same question opens.

## What must never happen

- A draw shown as always taking the likeliest token, or a slice not as wide as its probability.
- The replay changing anything but the drawn outcome: the question and the probabilities are identical.
- Low temperature shown as identical answers every time; temperature shown as mood or creativity.
- Sampling presented as the only reason answers differ, or as the cause of wrong answers.
- Any value presented as a real model's, or any visual implying it depicts ChatGPT's internals or settings.
